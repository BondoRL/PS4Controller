Play with your PlayStation controller.

Use a DualShock 4 or DualSense as an Xbox controller on Windows. Adjust your sticks, check your battery and keep the app up to date.

Download for Windows

Version 2.4.2 · 64-bit Windows · Web installer under 10 MB

Internet and administrator access are required. Setup downloads the app and any missing ViGEmBus and HidHide drivers.

Get started

  1. Download and run the installer.
  2. Restart Windows if setup asks you to.
  3. Connect your controller by USB or Bluetooth and start Xbox mode.
